# 🎵 MusicPlayer Console App

A simple **C# console application** that simulates a music player — manage your playlist, play your favorite songs, skip tracks, and control the volume — all from your terminal!

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎶 **Add Songs** — Add a new song by entering its title and artist.  
- ❌ **Remove Songs** — Delete a song from your playlist by title.  
- ▶️ **Play Music** — Play the currently selected song.  
- ⏭️ **Next / Previous** — Navigate easily between songs.  
- 📜 **View Playlist** — See all songs currently in your playlist.  
- 🔊 **Volume Control** — Adjust the volume (0–100).  
- 🧭 **Interactive Menu** — Simple console menu to control everything.

---

## 🧩 Classes Overview

### 🎧 `Song`
Represents a single song.

**Properties:**
- `Title` — The name of the song  
- `Artist` — The performer or band name

### 🎼 `MusicPlayer`
Manages the playlist and handles all core music operations.

**Main Functions:**
- `AddSong()` — Add a song to the playlist  
- `RemoveSong()` — Remove a song by title  
- `PlaySong()` — Play the current song  
- `NextSong()` / `PreviousSong()` — Navigate through songs  
- `SetVolume()` — Adjust the playback volume  
- `ShowPlaylist()` — Display all songs in the playlist  

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Start the Application**  
   Launch the app — an interactive menu will appear in the console.  

2. **Add Songs**  
   Enter the song title and artist to add it to your playlist.  

3. **Play Music**  
   Select “Play Current Song” to start playing.  

4. **Navigate Songs**  
   Choose “Next Song” or “Previous Song” to move between tracks.  

5. **Remove Songs**  
   Remove any song by typing its title.  

6. **Adjust Volume**  
   Set the volume level (0–100).  

7. **Exit**  
   Choose the “Exit” option to close the application.
